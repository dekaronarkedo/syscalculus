🛡️ Zero-Data Retention Architecture Guarantee

At SysCalculus (syscalculus.dev), we maintain a strict air-gapped client-side execution paradigm. All mathematical models, connection simulations, log scrubbing, regex processing, and code conversions occur strictly inside your web browser’s JavaScript V8/WASM engine. No log files, secrets, database credentials, or application telemetry are ever transmitted to our servers or stored on any external medium.

You can freely disconnect your internet connection or inspect browser telemetry in Network Developer Tools (F12) to verify that zero payload packets leave your workstation while utilizing our calculation workbenches.

⚖️ 2. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you are granted specific data protection rights under the GDPR. SysCalculus does not maintain user accounts, does not collect personal identifiers, and does not retain customer databases. However, with respect to analytics and advertising cookies:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data held by any third-party processor.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request erasure of your data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Object: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data for advertising purposes.

🏛️ 3. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose what personal information it collects, uses, and sells.

  • We Do Not Sell Personal Information: SysCalculus does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • Consumer Opt-Out: To exercise your CCPA rights or inquire about advertising identifier opt-outs, please consult our third-party ad settings or contact us directly.

🔒 4. Client-Side Cryptographic Log Redaction

Our Air-Gapped Log Sanitizer and systems engineering tools operate strictly via native RegExp and Web Crypto API primitives. When sensitive API keys, JWT tokens, IP addresses, or database passwords are scrubbed in the browser, all regex processing occurs purely within volatile client memory.

When you close or refresh the browser tab, all working buffers are immediately reclaimed by the browser's garbage collector. Zero persistence exists on disk or over HTTP.

📡 5. Log Files & Standard Web Server Telemetry

Like all web infrastructure networks, our edge hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare Anycast CDN) automatically collects standard non-personally identifiable log information when assets are requested. This includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser types, internet service providers (ISPs), referring/exit pages, and timestamps.

This telemetry is collected strictly for DDoS mitigation, edge routing optimization, and infrastructure capacity planning. No individual profiling or data harvesting is conducted.
